Ours start with code.

Rangers Coding Club is a coding club for younger students — a place to learn how computers actually work by building real little things: games, websites, animations, robots-in-spirit. This page is version 2 of the club's website: a cleaner, faster refresh of the first site I built for the club.

What we learn

Four tracks, picked by age and interest. Everybody starts at lesson one — nobody needs experience, and nobody gets left behind for asking questions. That's the whole rule.

What rangers build

Projects are the curriculum. These are the kinds of things members work toward — start with one, finish it, show it off on demo day, pick the next one.

A normal week at the club

Warm-up (10 min)A tiny puzzle or typing challenge to get brains on.
Lesson (25 min)One new concept, shown live, then everybody tries it.
Build time (40 min)Project work, with mentors floating to unstick people.
Show & tell (10 min)Two rangers demo what they made — finished or gloriously broken.
